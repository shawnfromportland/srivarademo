You had heard about Srivara only in historic legends, and now as a traveler, excited and curious, you are about to set foot upon it’s distant lands for the first time. Few visitors have ever made it out here this far.

The current inhabitants of the once-abundant lands of Srivara are mostly descendents of the original settlers. For nearly two centuries, fresh water flowed and the terraced gardens of the settlement were fertile.

But the people are in trouble now, as the world has gone into a drought super-cycle in recent years. Hear their stories, experience their culture, religion, and engineering. Determine your fate and possibly the fate of Srivara itself through your choices.

Hundreds of playthrough experiences possible, 8 death scenarios and 6 different endings, chosen by your decisions in Srivara. Unique character interactions, Minor puzzles, and no complex game mechanics, with a unique art style inspired by 1980s adventure gamebooks.

Srivara is designed to be fully playable as a non-linear graphic novel point-and-click game in both print and e-book formats, and may come to Steam eventually.
