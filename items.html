Items

There are items you can find and collect during your adventure in Srivara. These items might help you survive or unlock puzzles.

Keep track of which items you have found during your journey by making a copy of the attached items sheet and filling it out.

To prevent spoilers, items in the story may be referenced by number only, and you may be required to give the item’s code to pass some puzzles.

You begin with no items.
