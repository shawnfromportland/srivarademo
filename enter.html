How To Play

Srivara is not a linear book. This is a non-linear adventure experience that can lead to multiple different endings based on the actions you choose.

Do not read the book page by page – you will spoil the story and the puzzles.

On each page, continue the story by selecting one action from the choices and turning to the page number indicated.

If you make a fatal choice along your way, you will have to continue and try again from your previous save point. There are various save points throughout the adventure such as homes and camp sites.
